Slot widths, spacing, and fit assumptions

Apple’s slot map specifies double-wide positions 1–4, single-wide positions 5–7, and the half-length I/O position 8. This preview uses a configurable nominal 20.32 mm (0.8 in) bracket pitch: 40.64 mm between double-wide connector positions, then 20.32 mm between the upper single-wide positions. These are derived preview coordinates, not caliper measurements of this chassis.

Each card documents its published cooler thickness and slot count. Collision width is the larger of the measured cooler width and the published nominal slot envelope. Missing thickness uses the reported slot count × bracket pitch; missing slot count uses a two-slot estimate. Enter a measured thickness and length per card to resolve unknowns. A 0.1 mm tolerance handles rounding of 60.96 mm to 61 mm; it is not assembly clearance. Extra gap adds your desired separation.

MPX modules can occupy connector pairs 1–2 or 3–4. The builder reserves the whole pair, allows at most two modules, and scales the full-height module artwork to fill its reserved bay, matching the supplied two-module reference. Unpublished MPX body dimensions remain estimates until measured.

Length and slot checks do not establish electrical compatibility, power capacity, cooling adequacy, cable clearance, or clearance perpendicular to this view.
